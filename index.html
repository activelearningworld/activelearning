Active Learning ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of users of our Android applications. This Privacy Policy applies to all Android applications published by Active Learning on Google Play.

By using any of our applications, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, through support requests).
  • Non-Personal Information: We may collect non-identifiable data such as device type, operating system version, and usage patterns to improve our applications.
  • Device Information: We may collect information about your device, including hardware model, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.

2. Usage of Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services. This includes understanding user preferences, diagnosing technical issues, and enhancing overall user experience.

3. AdMob Advertising

Our applications may use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and use the Google Advertising ID to deliver personalized ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of interest-based ads at any time through your device's settings.

4. Analytics

We may use third-party analytics tools (such as Firebase Analytics) to understand how our applications are used. These tools collect anonymized usage data. We also use crash reporting tools to identify and fix technical issues.

5. Third-Party Services

Our applications may integrate third-party software development kits (SDKs) including:

  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements.
  • Google Play Services: For core functionality such as authentication and in-app updates.
  • Firebase: For analytics, crash reporting, and cloud messaging.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party SDKs.

🔒 Our Commitment: We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Your trust is our priority.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Where applicable, we comply with the GDPR and CCPA. To exercise your rights, please contact us.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
